Biometric Identification

The archive holds documents on biometric identification as a condition of assistance, with UNHCR’s identity-management guidance read as the node and four disputes from 2023 to 2026 read as its recontextualisations. The cases are Gaza, Malaysia, South Africa and Mizoram, and the textual analysis reads the 21 saved core texts at the first of Fairclough’s three dimensions.
